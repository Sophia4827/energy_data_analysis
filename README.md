# Energy Efficiency Analysis

This repo contains the analysis notebook for a data science project investigating energy efficiency in buildings. Taking the perspective of analysts hired by European architects, we explored which building design features most strongly influence heating and cooling loads, with the goal of informing more energy-efficient building designs that comply with Europe's legal energy mandates.

- [Analysis Notebook](./Final_Project_Jakob_Cheng,_Donny_Dutcher,_Sophia_Huang,_Kaitlyn_Ip.ipynb): Full pipeline including data exploration, UMAP clustering, linear regression, and random forest feature importance analysis

## Overview

Using a dataset of 768 simulated building configurations (generated by varying 8 design parameters in Ecotect), we investigated the relationship between building characteristics and their estimated energy requirements.

**Research Questions:**
1. How does relative compactness relate to heating and cooling loads? *(Data Visualization)*
2. Do similar buildings cluster together based on energy load profiles? *(UMAP)*
3. How accurately can we predict heating and cooling loads from design features? *(Linear Regression)*
4. Which features most strongly influence heating and cooling loads? *(Random Forest)*

## Key Findings

- **Relative compactness** was the most influential feature for predicting both heating and cooling loads — buildings with higher compactness (more cube-like) had lower energy loads due to reduced surface area exposed to the environment
- **Overall height** was the second most important feature for both targets
- **UMAP** revealed that including orientation and glazing area distribution produced more distinct clustering, suggesting these variables are important for energy load analysis
- **Linear regression** performed adequately (residual errors 2.7–3.15) but showed non-random residual patterns, suggesting random forest was a better fit for this data
- **Random forest** models confirmed relative compactness and overall height as dominant predictors

## Dataset

- **Source:** Tsanas & Xifara energy analysis simulation via Ecotect
- **768 samples**, 8 building design features, 2 target variables (Heating Load, Cooling Load)
- Features include: relative compactness, surface area, wall area, roof area, overall height, orientation, glazing area, glazing area distribution
